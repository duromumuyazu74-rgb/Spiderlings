## Reproduced defects and fixes

1. A MaidKnightHeavy stalled for forty native turns against a Spinner web while pursuing its owner. Owned web proxies now participate in Maidforce hostility and projectile collision; they no longer inherit IceWall's player-dependent low-priority exclusion. Allied NPC targeting reads the real `targetedForAttack` flag. Native melee opens and uses the breach on both games. Unrelated scenery and global faction relations are unchanged.
2. Recovery used the pulling spider's discounted web route, selecting a blocked diagonal corner instead of the player's open breach. It now tries the ordinary native obstacle route first, then a cardinal spider route for paid web crossings. Existing costs, locks, occupied landing checks and the one-pull-per-turn rule still apply.
3. Native `LoadGame` calls `AdvanceTime(0)`. Spinner's before-move work treated that zero as a default positive turn and spent saved credit on another operation. Zero-time refreshes now preserve position, credit and topology. The native construction snapshot is identical before and after reload.

## Native observations and balance

One, two, four and eight real Spinner workers all complete retained 3×3, 5×5 and 7×7 layers. Their traces separate travel, paid construction, waiting and yielding. Each configuration also takes native damage and pays to restore the breach. A lone Spinner can build; the existing two-participant player capture condition remains. Gate/capture and seven-stage art scenarios continue to pass.

Web travel remains faster without accelerating attacks or work. Native melee completes on turns 2, 4, …, 18 on both surfaces. One WebCaster pays wrapping work on turns 2, 3 and 5 on both surfaces. Thirty autonomous construction operations require forty-five stationary work opportunities in both runs. Total construction time differs with site selection and travel; it is not presented as an identical world-turn result.

Capable full-pin attacks deal 0.65 of matching unpinned damage: melee 3→1.95, HeavySlash 5.5→3.575 and HeadMaidHairpin 2→1.3. An attack arriving after native struggle restores initial adhesion correctly deals full damage. Native voluntary movement and ShadowShroud teleport are blocked while pinned; external displacement works. Saved attribution survives reload, eight active-map turns expire the pressure window, and native untying clears owned silk. Existing regressions cover inactive-floor clocks and shield/immune/passive-hit boundaries.

One spider needs three paid wrapping contributions; three ready adjacent spiders can finish those contributions in one turn. Stun, displacement, freeing and helplessness recovery interrupt work. A heavy enemy can kill the sole wrapping spider and stop the attempt. Cancelled native capture retries without another payment. Native death-first and capture-first ordering cannot produce two successful exits. Ordinary protected shops and `nocapture` prey remain excluded; the later Hunting Grounds prey rule is tested separately. Isolated lifecycle rows stun the target to control ordering; cooperation and retaliation rows leave native offense active.

The player breaks the test boundary with two unarmed native attacks. Compatible native collars/leashes admit 2/4/8 recovery sources. Stand firm costs 7/11/19 displayed stamina. Pulling through an open breach resumes after the resisted action. Two targeted native Remove attempts detach a source while retaining the carrier; the native carrier result can remain `Fail` because the whole restraint was deliberately not removed. External leash cut/struggle progress survives. The owned-carrier penalty remains 0.05 per extra source; source loss, distance, stun and targeted removal provide counterplay. These observations do not justify numeric retuning.

Hex gives two warning turns, three active turns, and marks lasting 3/4/5 turns as stacks rise. Native reload preserves its clock. Heavy-shield damage is 2 normally and 3 while fragile; at 0.5 shield, ordinary HP overflow stays 1.5 in both cases, with no extra fragility overflow. A three-stack native silk hit schedules a blast two turns later. Collapse shows 21 cells, hits on turn three, then starts a seven-turn cooldown. Three ordinary player moves leave its footprint in time, and removing the caster cancels the pending strike.

Hunting Grounds and Infestation generate separate 3- and 5-nest objectives. Cancelled deaths and later ordinary nests do not change the target count. Actual native stairs refuse descent before completion and reach floor 4 from floor 3 after the original objectives die. Thirty seeded Hunting Grounds maps per game cover floors 3, 7 and 12. The sustained ordinary-floor scene advances 310 positive native turns with construction, broken/repaired webs, new nests and five native captures. It ends with 46 physical cells, 46 unique proxies, one plan and no pending capture. A mid-run native save/reload preserves state; no Hunting-only objective leaks into this ordinary scene.
